FAQ

Questions life-science marketing teams ask us

What is Exhibit RX?

Exhibit RX is a trade show management partner for pharma and life-science companies. We can manage your entire exhibition program or step in to support one show or one challenge, from planning to performance.

Can you help with just one part of our show?

Yes. Many clients start with The Right Room, our pre-show meetings program, then build from there. We scope each engagement to what you need.

Do you manage booth builders and vendors?

Yes. We coordinate the builders and vendors you already use, and can help you find new ones when you need them.

Do you guarantee meetings or leads?

No. Results depend on your audience, offer and timing. We agree measures of success up front and report against them, so you always see progress.

How do you book meetings before a show?

We work within each event's rules and the access you have. We don't share event logins, pull restricted attendee data or send automated mass outreach. You see exactly what's happening every week.

Do you support trade shows outside the US?

Yes. We support pharma and life-science teams at shows in Europe, the US and beyond.

What is the Exhibit RX Method?

Five stages we run for every show: Prescribe, Prepare, Perform, Prove and Progress. It starts by defining what success looks like and ends with a post-show health check and improvements that make the next show better.

How much does Exhibit RX cost?

Each engagement is scoped to your shows, timeline and goals. Start with a complimentary 30-minute Show Diagnosis. You'll leave with three priorities for your next show, and we'll recommend the right path and share pricing.

Does Exhibit RX replace our booth builder, CRM or sales team?

No. We work alongside your booth vendor, inside your CRM and with your sales and technical teams. Technical questions, pricing and product claims stay with your own people.

Does Exhibit RX use AI?

Yes, behind the scenes, to move faster. We use AI tools to research target accounts, draft outreach, turn booth notes into clean lead records and build your post-show summary. People make the calls: who to meet, which leads matter and who owns the next step. You get the speed without handing your show to a machine.
