FAQ

Questions life-science marketing teams ask us

What is Exhibit RX?

Exhibit RX is the trade-show performance partner for pharma and life sciences companies. We define what success looks like before the show, run the plan with your team, get every lead to an owner in your CRM, and report what the show produced at 30, 60 and 90 days.

What is the Exhibit RX Method?

Five stages we run for every show: Prescribe, Prepare, Perform, Prove and Progress. It starts by defining success, including how much of the show should go to new business, and ends with recommendations that make the next show better.

What is RX Continuum?

For companies with four or more shows a year. Every show still runs through the Method, but your setup, systems and lessons carry over, so each show starts ahead of the last.

How fast do leads reach our sales team?

We set the routing timeline with you before the show, based on your CRM and your team. Priority leads reach the right rep while the conversation is still fresh.

How much does Exhibit RX cost?

Each show is scoped to your timeline and goals. Book a free 20-minute Show Diagnosis and we'll share pricing for your show.

Does Exhibit RX replace our booth builder or our sales team?

No. We coordinate with your builder and vendors and work inside your CRM. Technical questions, pricing and product claims stay with your own sales and technical team.
