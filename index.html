FAQ

Questions life-science marketing teams ask us

What is Exhibit RX?

Exhibit RX is the trade-show performance partner for life-science companies. We connect strategy, preparation, execution and measurement so your trade shows run as coordinated commercial programs, not isolated events.

What is the Exhibit RX Method?

Five stages we run for every show: Prescribe, Prepare, Perform, Prove and Progress. It starts by defining what success looks like and ends with a post-show health check and improvements that make the next show better.

How much does Exhibit RX cost?

Each engagement is scoped to your shows, timeline and goals. Start with a free 20-minute Show Diagnosis and we'll recommend the right path and share pricing.

Does Exhibit RX replace our booth builder, CRM or sales team?

No. We work alongside your booth vendor, inside your CRM and with your sales and technical teams. Technical questions, pricing and product claims stay with your own people.
