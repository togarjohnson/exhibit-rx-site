The problem

Trade shows are expensive. The work around them is often fragmented.

Marketing manages logistics. Sales manages relationships. Technical teams join meetings. The booth vendor manages the exhibit. A lead-capture app collects contacts. The CRM stores the data. Nobody necessarily owns the complete performance of the show, from objective to outcome.

Unclear objectives, disconnected teams and meeting-scheduling chaos before the show even opens.

Inconsistent lead capture, weak qualification and unclear ownership once the doors are open.

Slow follow-up, limited measurement, and the same mistakes repeated at the next show.

The Exhibit RX difference

Your booth is only one part of the show.

The booth gets built. Exhibit RX helps make the investment perform. Keep your CRM, your sales process, your booth vendor, and the systems and partners that already work. We connect the pieces surrounding the trade show so your organization gets more value from the investment it's already making.
