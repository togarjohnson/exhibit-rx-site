Exhibitor portal coordination

One point of accountability for the exhibitor portal.

Within Fractional Show Management, Exhibit RX serves as the primary coordination point for your trade show exhibitor portal: reviewing organizer requirements, tracking deadlines, maintaining exhibitor profile information, coordinating booth staff registration, and flagging missing information, approvals and required payments.

Exhibit RX may enter information supplied or approved by your team, your exhibit builder, vendors, sales or technical staff. You remain responsible for approving product claims, technical specifications, regulatory information, pricing, legal statements and final marketing claims. Exhibit RX does not finance show fees, booth build, sponsorships, site services, lead retrieval purchases, shipping, freight, electrical, rigging or other vendor costs; any required purchase is funded or paid directly by you.

FAQ

Questions life-science marketing teams ask us

What is Exhibit RX?

Exhibit RX is the trade-show performance partner for life-science companies. We connect strategy, preparation, execution, lead intelligence and measurement around the trade shows you already invest in, using the Exhibit RX Method: Prescribe, Prepare, Perform, Prove, Progress.

Is Exhibit RX a booth builder or event agency?

No. Exhibit RX doesn't build booths, plan logistics or scan badges on its own. We're the performance layer that connects the people and systems already doing those things, so the investment performs commercially.

Will Exhibit RX replace our CRM, sales process or booth vendor?

No. Keep your CRM, your sales process and your booth vendor. Exhibit RX connects the pieces surrounding the trade show so your organization gets more value from the systems and partners you already trust.

How is Exhibit RX different from an event agency or a lead retrieval app?

Event agencies manage booths and logistics. Lead retrieval apps scan badges. Exhibit RX owns the performance of the show end to end: objectives, preparation, onsite coordination, lead intelligence and post-show measurement.

Which trade shows does Exhibit RX support?

Exhibit RX focuses on life-science trade shows in the US and Europe, including CPHI, AAPS PharmSci 360, Interphex and the BIO International Convention, plus smaller regional and scientific meetings.

Which CRMs does Exhibit RX work with?

Exhibit RX routes trade-show leads into Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics and similar CRMs, working within the systems you already use rather than requiring a new one.

What is RX Pulse?

RX Pulse is Exhibit RX's post-show health check. It helps your team understand what happened, what needs attention, what worked, what didn't, and what should happen next. It sits inside the Prove stage of the Exhibit RX Method.

What is Exhibit RX Continuum?

Exhibit RX Continuum is the ongoing, multi-show layer of Exhibit RX. It connects insights, institutional knowledge and performance learning from one event into the next, so companies running several shows a year aren't starting from zero each time. It's a managed service, not a software platform.

How far before a trade show should we start?

Earlier is better, because the Prescribe and Prepare stages are where the most commercial value gets built. If your show is close, Exhibit RX can still help, focused on the highest-value pieces that remain possible to influence.

How much does Exhibit RX cost?

Engagements are scoped based on which solution fits, your show calendar, and how much runway remains before your show. Booth space, build, shipping and travel are billed at cost. Get a free Show Diagnosis to get pricing for your situation.

How does Exhibit RX handle GDPR at European shows like CPHI?

Consent is recorded at the point of capture, only the data follow-up needs is collected, and every client signs a data processing agreement. The client owns the leads, and lead data is deleted or returned on a schedule set in the contract.
