Trade Show Portal Administration

One point of accountability for the exhibitor portal.

Exhibit RX serves as the primary coordination point for your trade show exhibitor portal within the agreed program scope — reviewing organizer requirements, tracking deadlines, maintaining exhibitor profile information, coordinating booth staff registration, approved descriptions and listings, lead retrieval and sponsorship coordination, and flagging missing information, approvals and required payments.

Exhibit RX may enter information supplied or approved by your team, your exhibit builder, vendors, sales or technical staff. You remain responsible for approving product claims, technical specifications, regulatory information, pricing, legal statements and final marketing claims. Exhibit RX does not finance show fees, booth build, sponsorships, site services, lead retrieval purchases, shipping, freight, electrical, rigging or other vendor costs — any required purchase is funded or paid directly by you. Portal administration stays within the agreed program scope.

How we talk about routing and timing

An implementation standard, not a public guarantee.

Exhibit RX establishes the lead capture, qualification and handoff workflow during pre-show implementation. Specific CRM routing and handoff timing is set based on your CRM environment, show-provided lead retrieval tools, required integrations, system access and approval requirements — because that timing depends on infrastructure Exhibit RX does not fully control (the show organizer, lead retrieval providers, CRM administrators, your IT team, and other third parties). Where appropriate, your proposal or SOW may establish an individual service target after discovery, for example: a target of qualified leads routed within one business day where the agreed workflow, system access and required data are available. That target is set per client, not promised as a universal website guarantee, and Exhibit RX does not offer a public money-back guarantee at this stage.
