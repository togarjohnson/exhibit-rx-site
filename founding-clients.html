Founding Client Program

Help shape how Exhibit RX works, from the start.

We're inviting a small group of pharma and life-science companies to work with us as founding clients. You get senior, hands-on attention on a clearly scoped first engagement. We get your candid feedback and, if you're happy with the results, the chance to tell that story together.

Explore the Founding Client Program
What founding clients receive
  • Introductory terms on a defined first engagement
  • Deliverables and scope agreed in writing before we start
  • Direct involvement from the founder throughout
  • Structured check-ins to shape the work as it happens
  • The option to continue into longer-term services
What we ask in return
  • Structured feedback at agreed points
  • Permission to measure outcomes with you
  • Consideration of a testimonial or case study, only with your approval
A good fit

Built for lean teams with real show calendars.

  • You exhibit at several life-science shows a year
  • You run events with a lean marketing team
  • You have a show in the next few months you want to get more from

A natural first engagement is The Right Room by Exhibit RX, our focused pre-show meetings program, or support for a single show.

How it works

Four simple steps.

  1. Book a callTell us about your shows and goals.
  2. Agree the scopeWe define the engagement and deliverables in writing.
  3. Run the engagementWe do the work, with check-ins along the way.
  4. Review results togetherWe measure outcomes and decide what's next.
