The real problem

The money isn't lost at the booth. It's lost after.

A flagship show can cost a quarter million dollars or more. Most of it quietly goes to dinners and meetings with customers you already have. Your technical people are booked with existing accounts, so new prospects get whoever is free. Leadership sees a booth bill and a badge count, never the opportunities the show created. Then scans sit in a spreadsheet and follow-up depends on which rep remembers, while the 30, 60 and 90 days after the show are what actually decide the return.
