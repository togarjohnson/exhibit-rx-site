Why Exhibit RX exists

Companies spend heavily on trade shows but rarely have a connected process around them.

The problem

Pre-show, onsite, qualification, CRM, sales and follow-up usually live in separate hands.

Pre-show activity, booth conversations, lead qualification, the CRM, sales ownership, follow-up and measurement are frequently owned by different people, with no one connecting the handoffs between them. That's where trade show value usually leaks.

Why Exhibit RX exists

Togar experienced this problem from inside the pharma industry.

Exhibit RX was created to close that gap — not by building another point tool, but by owning the commercial process that connects the ones you already have.

Experience from inside the industry

Founder Togar Johnson

Togar previously worked as a Global Marketing Manager at a global pharma ingredients company, where his responsibilities included global trade shows, builder coordination, pre-show meeting programs, lead capture, digital marketing, CRM handoff and follow-up. At a single major show, that program produced 80+ pre-booked meetings and 160+ leads.

Before pharma, Togar spent more than five years as Director of Marketing and Communications for the 436th Force Support Squadron at Dover Air Force Base — running 50+ events, including an air show with 150,000+ attendees over three days and approximately $200,000 in sponsorships.
