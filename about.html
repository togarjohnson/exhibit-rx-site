Built from inside life-science trade-show programs

Companies spend heavily on trade shows but rarely have one partner owning how they perform.

The problem

Objectives, preparation, execution, lead intelligence and measurement usually live in separate hands.

Marketing manages logistics. Sales manages relationships. Technical teams join meetings. The booth vendor manages the exhibit. A lead-capture app collects contacts. The CRM stores the data. No one necessarily owns the complete performance of the show, from objective to outcome. That's where trade-show value usually leaks.

Why Exhibit RX exists

Exhibit RX was created from firsthand experience managing complex global trade-show programs.

Not by building another point tool, but by owning the commercial performance that connects the tools, teams and vendors you already have. Exhibit RX is a structured methodology and a partner, not one person standing in for your whole program.

Exhibit RX's methodology was built to close those gaps.
