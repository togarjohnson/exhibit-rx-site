Why Exhibit RX exists.

Exhibit RX was created from firsthand experience managing complex global pharma trade-show programs, and seeing the gap between executing an event well and getting its full commercial value.

Experience behind the method

Founder Togar G. Johnson

"I've walked the floor at major industry events like CPHI, SupplySide Global and World Vaccine Congress. The booths were packed, but the pipeline wasn't. I watched great conversations disappear because no one owned what happened next. I built Exhibit RX to close that gap."

Togar G. Johnson, Founder

Togar G. Johnson has spent 20 years in marketing across federal, commercial, pharmaceutical and entrepreneurial roles. He has run or hosted more than 75 trade shows and events, up to 20 a year around the world. As Global Marketing Manager in pharma ingredients and vaccines, he ran global trade shows, builder coordination, pre-show meeting programs, lead capture and CRM handoff from inside the industry.

Before that, he spent more than five years as Marketing Director at Dover Air Force Base, delivering 50+ events, including the Thunder Over Dover air show and a Tori Kelly and Lecrae concert.
