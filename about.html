Why Exhibit RX exists.

Exhibit RX was built from years of running pharma trade show programs with a lean team. The same problems showed up every year: too many vendors, too little time, and good conversations that disappeared after the show. Exhibit RX exists to fix that, from the first planning call to the last follow-up.

Experience behind the method

What I saw from the inside

Lean teams carry heavy shows.

Most life-science companies invest heavily in trade shows and staff them with marketing teams of two or three people. Those teams manage builders, shipping, budgets, sales calendars, technical experts and lead follow-up, on top of their day jobs. The shows still happen. What gets lost is the commercial value: the meetings that never got booked and the leads no one owned.
