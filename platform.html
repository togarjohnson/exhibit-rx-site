Exhibit RX Platform Preview

The technology layer supporting the Exhibit RX trade show revenue process.

Exhibit RX is a trade show revenue operations company, not primarily a software company — at this stage, the service is the product, and the platform supports delivery and creates future scalability.

Built around real relationships

The platform exists to support the conversation, not replace it.

Reps still do the relationship-building onsite. Exhibit RX makes sure every meeting booked, lead captured and promise made converts into a tracked next step, instead of a business card left in a pocket. Founding clients may run on a mix of Calendly, HubSpot Meetings, official show lead retrieval systems, business-card capture, CSV files, Zapier, Salesforce, HubSpot or other client CRMs while the native platform matures.

Available / supported today

What actually works right now.

We don't imply a feature is live unless it actually works. This is the live Exhibit RX Platform, used alongside the tools above as needed.

Client portfolio dashboard

See every show across the portfolio in one place, with role-based access for admins, reps, technical SMEs and executive viewers.

Show setup + meeting links

Create a show, assign reps, and generate personalized meeting booking links with location scheduling.

Lead capture + qualification

Standardized capture fields, 3–5 client-specific qualification questions, and A/B/C scoring applied consistently.

Lead queue + owner assignment

Review captured leads, assign owners, and flag exceptions that need routing.

Follow-up tracking

Track follow-up tasks and completion status per lead.

CSV import / export

Manual fallback for lead retrieval and CRM handoff while native integrations are built.

Native Exhibit RX development roadmap

Pricing

Bundled into the service today.

At launch, platform access is bundled into your Exhibit RX program — it isn't sold as a standalone subscription yet. The long-term concept is roughly $400–$800/month per team once the platform independently provides meaningful capabilities like multi-show management, meeting coordination, CRM connectivity and pipeline reporting.
