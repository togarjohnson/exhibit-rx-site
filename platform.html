Exhibit RX Continuum™

One show should make the next one better.

Exhibit RX Continuum is the managed layer that connects your trade-show program across the whole year, not a software platform you log into. It carries strategy, insights and institutional knowledge from one event into the next.

Built around real relationships

Continuum supports the program. It doesn't replace the people running it.

Your team and reps still build the relationships onsite. Continuum makes sure what's learned at one show, what worked, what didn't, which accounts mattered, follows your program into the next show instead of starting over. It works alongside your existing CRM, booth vendor and lead-capture tools.

Stop treating every show like an isolated project

What Continuum connects across your event calendar.

Institutional knowledge

What worked and what didn't at each show carries forward, instead of living only in one person's memory.

Target-account continuity

Accounts and conversations that matter at one show stay visible going into the next.

Consistent standards

The same qualification, routing and follow-up standards apply across every show in your program.

RX Pulse, compounded

Each post-show health check feeds the next show's Prescribe stage, instead of ending as a one-time report.

Portfolio-level visibility

Leadership can see performance across the full event calendar, not just one show at a time.

Smarter investment decisions

Which shows are worth the spend becomes a data-informed decision, not a repeated guess.
