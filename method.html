The Exhibit RX Method

Attract. Capture. Connect. Convert.

One commercial process built around the trade show. Before, during and after are timing labels underneath this framework — the method itself is what stays consistent from show to show.

4Stages: Attract, Capture, Connect, Convert
3Timing windows: Before, During, After
0Leads without an owner or next action
Attract Capture Connect Convert
ATTRACT · Before the show

Fill the calendar before the doors open.

We build and run the pre-show meeting campaign that puts the right meetings and conversations on the calendar before the doors open. We don't promise a specific number of meetings — we build and run the campaign that earns them.

May include: target-account planning, meeting outreach strategy, bookable meeting links, pre-show email and LinkedIn activity, show messaging, booth traffic strategy.

CAPTURE · During the show

Capture the context behind every meaningful conversation.

Badge or business-card capture, conversation notes, 3 to 5 client-specific qualification questions, A/B/C lead scoring, immediate identification of high-priority opportunities, and clear next-step information for every lead.

A LeadActive project or timeline, plus decision-making role.
B LeadReal interest but no defined timeline.
C LeadInformation gathering, students, competitors, or low priority.
CONNECT · During → After the show

Connect qualified conversations to your sales process.

CRM field mapping, lead ownership, territory assignment, product assignment, import/sync workflows, duplicate cleanup, sales handoff, follow-up ownership and lead-routing visibility.

Specific CRM routing and handoff timing is set during pre-show implementation based on your CRM environment, required integrations, system access and show-provided lead retrieval tools — not promised as a universal 24-hour guarantee. See Programs & Pricing for how an individual service target can be set in your SOW.

CONVERT · After the show

Support timely follow-up and measure what happens next.

We help support timely follow-up and measure what progresses after the show: contacts, qualified conversations, meetings, sales conversations, opportunities, pipeline influenced, and follow-up completion.

Reporting cadence: 30, 60 and 90 days, depending on the selected program.

Why one method, not just steps

Most teams already own a piece of this. Few teams own the handoffs.

Marketing owns Attract. Booth staff own Capture. Sales and the CRM own Connect. Leadership owns Convert. Exhibit RX is the connective layer between them, so nothing gets dropped at the handoff.
