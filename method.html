The Exhibit RX Method

Prescribe. Prepare. Perform. Prove. Progress.

A structured, repeatable methodology for trade-show performance. Exhibit RX diagnoses what's preventing a show from delivering its full commercial value, prescribes the right strategy, coordinates execution, proves what happened, and uses those insights to improve the next show.

5Stages: Prescribe, Prepare, Perform, Prove, Progress
1Partner owning the full performance arc
0Systems or vendors you have to replace
Prescribe Prepare Perform Prove Progress
1 · PRESCRIBE

Define what success should look like.

Before anything is built or booked, we diagnose what's standing in the way of your show performing commercially, and define what success actually looks like for this event. This is the strategic foundation every other stage is built on.

2 · PREPARE

Align the people, priorities and plan before the show.

We align the people, priorities, systems, meetings and execution plan before the show opens, so your team, your booth vendor and your CRM are all pointed at the same objective on day one.

3 · PERFORM

Coordinate execution as the event unfolds.

Exhibit RX coordinates execution, maintains visibility across every moving piece, and solves problems in real time as the show unfolds, so nothing falls through the cracks between teams.

4 · PROVE

Turn event activity into meaningful insight.

We turn what happened at the show into insight leadership can actually use, showing what worked, what didn't, and what the event produced. This is where RX Pulse, the post-show health check, lives.

5 · PROGRESS

Make the next show better than this one.

We use what was learned to improve the next show and the broader event program, so insight doesn't evaporate the day the booth comes down. For companies running multiple shows a year, this is carried forward through Exhibit RX Continuum.

Why one method, not just steps

Most teams already own a piece of this. Few teams own the whole arc.

Marketing often owns Prescribe and Prepare. Booth staff live inside Perform. Leadership wants Prove. Almost nobody is responsible for Progress. Exhibit RX is the connective layer across all five, so nothing gets dropped at the handoff.
